# small plain text at its right, after Save's status and the facts — and a
# tap opens the same reading speed and target popover. The strip draws no
# length label of its own, full or minimized. Nothing in the header overlaps
# (bounding boxes), nothing is wider than the screen, and the status line's
# items stay on their line. Its own suite: device metrics are set once.
#
