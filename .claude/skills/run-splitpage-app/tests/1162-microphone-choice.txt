# Choosing the microphone Follow My Voice listens on (issue #1162), in the
# teleprompter and in Settings, from one stored choice.
#
# The browser's recognizer has no device setting; it takes whatever audio track
# start(track) is given. So the choice is a deviceId stored under
# splitpage-microphone: Follow My Voice asks getUserMedia for that device
# (exact) and hands the track to the recognizer. Here enumerateDevices,
# getUserMedia and the recognizer's start/stop are replaced, as in
# tests/83-voice-mic-name.txt, so no microphone or speech service is touched.
#
# Every `eval` is one physical line (the driver splits on newlines).
