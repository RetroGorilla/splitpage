# A picture in a scene's gallery dialog at full size (#216,
# 05e-gallery-overlay.js): a click on the picked picture in the preview (or
# Enter on it) lays it over the whole window —
#   * bigger than the window: fitted first; a click or the wheel takes it to
#     100 % round the point under the pointer, a drag pans, a click goes back;
#   * smaller: at 100 %, never blown up, and a click on it does nothing;
#   * a readout of its real dimensions and its name;
#   * ← / → step through the scene's pictures (not its videos), the dialog
#     picking along; Delete does nothing here;
#   * Esc, the × or a click on the backdrop go back to the dialog with the
#     same item picked and the focus on the preview;
#   * a video's still is not expanded; a review session can still expand;
#   * no network request, no undo entry, the document unchanged.
# Every `eval` is one physical line.
