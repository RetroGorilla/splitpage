# A side panel opening or closing keeps your place (bug hunt, 2026-09-25,
# 03b-side-panels.js holdScriptPlace / sidePanelOpening).
#
# From 1100px a panel pushes the script over (body.panel-open #root's
# padding), every row rewraps taller, and the browser's scroll anchoring
# does not step in for a padding change: at 1440x800 with 60 scenes,
# scrolled to 4000, the scene at the top of the view went to 1700px below
# it. Now the first line of the scene at the top of the view stays put,
# through the slide as well as after it, the way the script zoom (#191)
# keeps its place. Checked here:
#   * opening Comments, then closing it: the first scene starting below the
#     header keeps its top to 3px in every frame after the click (sampled
#     after each frame's own work, so what was painted)
#   * switching to a panel of another width (Production dragged to 700px)
#   * at the top of the page nothing scrolls
#   * scrolling yourself during the slide is not fought
#   * the toolbar does not step sideways as the page's scroll bar goes and
#     comes back (#187 kept: no page bar while a panel is open)
#   * a toast rises clear of the gallery's full-size caption (#216)
#
# Every `eval` is one physical line.
