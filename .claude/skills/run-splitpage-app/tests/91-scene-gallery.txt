# A scene's Image section is a gallery (#135; 02d-gallery.js for the
# model, rowGalleryHtml in 05-render.js for the cell, 05e-gallery-overlay.js
# for its dialog), drawn as Adam's chosen design:
#   * in the cell, a capped mosaic: 1 item is the picture as before; 3 and 4
#     are a cover spanning three rows with the rest beside it; 5 and 10 show
#     the cover, items 2 and 3 and a "+N" button tile holding the 4th
#     item's picture; a video's tile has a play badge (big on the cover,
#     with its length);
#   * under it one line per video — host, then the link — at most three,
#     then "+N more videos"; a line opens the video (window.open in the
#     browser, the bridge's openExternal on desktop);
#   * any tile opens the dialog at that item: "Scene N · Visual", the
#     counts, a preview with ‹ › and "n / m", Open / Make cover / Remove, a
#     numbered grid with "behind +N" after item 3 and a "+" tile; "+N more
#     videos" opens it on the videos alone;
#   * Make cover, Remove (Delete), a move (Shift+→, or a drag) and an add
#     are each one undo step with a toast whose Undo takes it back; ← / →
#     pick; Esc closes;
#   * a .splitpage save carries every picture inline and reopens the same.
# The model, merge and exports are src/test/gallery.test.mjs; pastes and
# drops are 92-paste-into-gallery.txt. Every `eval` is one physical line.
