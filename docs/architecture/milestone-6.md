### 6.2 Undo replay as a transaction

- **Scope.** `undo()` and `redo()` in `12a-undo.js` stop writing `S.state` directly through `applyStepsLive` and pass through the document-mutation boundary as a transaction kind of its own. The undo algorithm, diffing and inversion stay.
- **Commit contract.**
  - Replay, row stamps and dependent reconciliation execute inside the mutation boundary.
  - An accepted replay advances the generation once, publishes once and records no new ordinary undo entry.
  - An empty or refused replay has no document edit effects.
  - A failure before the commit leaves the document and the history consistent. A failure after it is reported as committed and does not roll the document back.
  - The change set covers owner re-pointing and cleanup (#402, #494), including lists the original entry did not carry and the `docField` steps recorded into the entry.
- **Today.** `commit` in `document-session.ts` advances the generation, records an undo entry through `ports.undo.record` and publishes `"edit"`, unconditionally. Using it for a replay would record the replay again. Wrapping only `applyStepsLive` would leave the stamps (`21-events.js`, after replay) outside the boundary and could omit indirect owner writes from rollback and change reporting. Today the replay reaches the session only afterwards, as `afterUndoApplied`'s `markDirty`, then renders and settles from `21-events.js`.
- **Preserved.** Refusal and history behaviour, grouping, coalescing, `forget`, the limit of 200, and the typing additions (coalesce-back drops, redo given back, composition marks).
- **Acceptance table: every undo route.**

| Route | Today | Must reach | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Keyboard shortcuts (Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Shift+Z, Ctrl+Y) | `21-events.js` keydown, native controls and draft boxes distinguished | the replay transaction | a keyboard case per shortcut; one gesture processed once |
| `historyUndo`, `historyRedo` actions | action handlers calling `undo()`/`redo()` | the replay transaction | action-route case |
| `beforeinput` undo and redo | the two `beforeinput` routes | the replay transaction | both input types, one gesture one replay |
| Toast Undo | direct `undo()` call | the replay transaction | toast case, including after a refused replay |
| Writer close and reopen (Ctrl+Y reopen, `undoSerial`) | `19-writer.js` | the replay transaction; reopen rule unchanged | existing Writer undo cases |
| Comment and reply draft undo (INV-55) | the box's own history | stays local; never the document's | `draft-edit.test.mjs`, `81-draft-edit.txt` |

- **Tests.** Fail-first cases for: generation advances once; one publication; no new entry in the undo or redo stack beyond the move; an empty replay and a refused replay change nothing observable; a pre-commit throw leaves document and history as before; a post-commit throw is reported and the document stays committed; re-pointed owners appear in the change set; the existing undo-owner, lifetime, draft and differential suites pass unchanged.

## As built

### 6.2 Undo replay as a transaction

- **Boundary.** `DocumentSession.replay` runs a `Replay` (plan, apply, settle) as transaction kind `"replay"`. Plan reads; apply writes the replayed steps and the row stamps; attribution signs it as for an edit; anchor reconciliation runs when the change set needs it; settle moves the history entry and resynchronizes the undo base. Any throw up to and including settle rolls the document back through the same rollback point as a command, and the history has not moved. Commit then advances the generation once, runs one effect pass and publishes once, and records no undo entry. A throw after commit comes back as `committed` and is reported.
- **Undo module.** `undo()` and `redo()` in `12a-undo.js` build the replay from `history.prepare`, which plans without writing (`planStepsLive`) and declares the change set: each replayed cell with its stamps, inserted, removed and moved rows, every document field the plan writes (including owner lists cleaned up or re-pointed that the entry did not carry), and `runsMoved` when comment anchors in a replayed cell change. The session bridge routes replays to the live session with its stamp ports. A refusal still drops the entry, as before. A failure before commit is rethrown to the caller.
- **Ledger rows taken (5).** Receiving owner: `mountUndoRoutes` in `src/features/undo/routes.ts`, mounted once from `21-events.js` with `#rows` and `#writer-doc` as roots. Window keydown (bubble) for document undo outside the roots, respecting native controls, draft boxes and review mode; `beforeinput` (bubble) on both roots for script `historyUndo`/`historyRedo`; the guarded Toast Undo (`undoIfLatest`); and the undo-hook installation (sent-comment predicate, refusal warning, the after-replay render and caret adapter). Precedence: the window route ignores targets inside the roots, so a gesture in a cell is handled once by `onRootKeydown`, which calls the owner's `key`. Disposer: `dispose()` removes the three registrations and releases the installed hooks. The after-replay adapter in `21-events.js` keeps render, production render, base settle and caret restore; stamping and the dirty mark moved into the transaction.
- **Evidence.** `tests/application/session-replay.test.ts` (the commit contract at the session), `src/test/undo-replay-app.test.mjs` (every document undo route in the booted app, stamps, refusal, pre- and post-commit failure, owner cleanup in the change set) and `tests/features/undo/routes.test.ts` (route precedence, one gesture one replay, Toast Undo guard, dispose and remount).

### 6.3 `foldIntoEdit`

- **Boundary.** `foldIntoEdit` in `12a-undo.js` runs its amendment through `DocumentSession.amend`, transaction kind `"amend"`, with the same plan, apply and settle shape and the same rollback point as a replay. Plan reads: it checks the ownership guard (`history.standing`: refused when a later entry is about the same fields, or the entry is no longer done), runs the caller's change on a copy and turns the difference into steps, so the change set (the written fields with their row stamps) is declared before anything is written. Apply writes the steps and the stamps; settle computes the new undo base first, then amends the entry (or, with no entry about it, takes the change in without one), then sets the base. Commit advances the generation once, runs one effect pass (dirty, draft, save) and publishes once as `"amend"`, and records no undo entry. A throw before commit rolls the document back with the entry unamended and is rethrown to the caller.

### 6.5 Records written by the command that references them (#325 replacement, first PR)

- **Command.** `cellTextCommand` takes the records an edit references (`CellRecords`: tags as category and name, b-roll shots as id and seed) and writes them in the same transaction as the cell. Plan works out which are new against the document (`newRegistryTag` for a tag, an unknown id for a shot) and declares `productionTags` or `brollShots` in the change set only when one is; apply pushes them before the text and the stamps. So an accepted edit is still one generation, one undo record and one publication; a refused, no-op or failed edit writes no record, and a failure rolls the registry and the shot list back with the cell. Without edit capability an edit that would add a record is refused, even when only its comment anchors differ.
- **Callers.** `editCell` passes the `records` its callback returns. The three callbacks that wrote a record before the command now return it instead: `tagTextRange` (the typed `[tag]` shortcut, the ghost completion and the untagged mention on Tab, in the grid and the Writer), `closePlainTag` (Tab) and `maybeCaptureBroll` (typing `B-roll:`). `registerProductionTag` and `newBrollRecord` keep their other callers, the panels and bulk tagging, which still write through `markDirty` in one legacy edit with the text they reference (6.8.1, 6.8.2).
- **Already in their command.** The cell paste (`pasteCommand`) and the scene paste (`recordPastedBroll` inside `pasteScenesCommand`'s apply) write their b-roll and tag records in the apply; the import scans run inside `importRowsCommand` (M5). Nothing changed there.
- **Pictures.** Picture and thumbnail bytes (the gallery picker, Retry thumbnail, annotations, the video thumbnail after a paste, pasted and dropped files, inline pictures the sanitiser interns, the scene clipboard, imports and DOCX) are interned in the content-addressed image store before their command, as they must be for the document to reference them. The store is shared by every document, draft and history version and has no delete, so bytes left by a refused command are the same as bytes left by a removed picture: no document record points at nothing and nothing in the document is orphaned. Collecting unreferenced bytes is a storage concern outside M6.
- **Left for the second PR.** Repair-on-read (`brollEntries()` with no argument, the tag registry heal on render, and `settleDerivedRecords` in save preparation) is unchanged. It still creates a shot whenever an unkeyed `B-roll:` line is read, which the new test works around and the second PR removes.
- **Evidence.** `src/test/orphan-records-app.test.mjs` in the booted app: the typed tag, the Tab-closed plain tag and the `B-roll:` capture each write their record in their one command (one generation, one publication declaring the registry or the shot list, undone with the text), and each leaves no record when the session refuses it and when its command fails (9 of 12 fail on the base); a refused media paste with a picture and a b-roll key and a refused import with a tracked b-roll line leave no record, against an allowed paste as the control. The 6.5 cases in `tests/application/cell-text.test.ts`: records inside the transaction and declared, a known record not written again or declared, no record for a no-op, refusal without edit capability, rollback on failure.

### 6.5 Repair-on-read removed (second PR)

- **Where records are kept now.** Entry (`migrateRows`, so every adoption, reload, import and incoming merge) registers live tags, as before, and now also gives each named `B-roll:` line a record and renames the record a line matches (`planBrollRecords` and `applyBrollRecords` in `core/production/broll.ts`, scope "every line"). Every session edit transaction, and every legacy `noteEdit`, asks the reconcile port's new `records` hook (`derivedRecordsPlan` in `08c-broll.js`, handed to the bridge with `provideRecordsReconciler`) for the records the edited document needs: tag registration and cue retitles (`tagRecordsPlan`, the old heal split into plan and apply), keyed-line records and renames, and records for unkeyed lines in inserted scenes or a whole-document change. The plan's fields join the change set, the apply runs after the edit and before the anchor reconcile, and a throw after it rolls the records back with the edit. Replays and amends do not run it: they restore what the history recorded. Leaving a cell keys its typed `B-roll:` lines through one `set-cell-text` command, which creates their records in the same transaction.
- **Removed.** The heal in `render()` and in the idle production refresh, the heal inside the panels' `trackedEntry` (which now registers inside the action's own legacy edit), the repairing `brollEntries()` (the badge, the unshot count, the b-roll panel and page, the export kit; it now only reads and its argument is gone), the idle `maybeAnchorBroll` in `runBrollRefresh`, and `settleDerivedRecords` in `writeDoc`. Save preparation is read-only; the bytes it writes are still the entry form of the held document, as before, because the export kit migrates its copy.
- **Behaviour.** An unkeyed line in a scene nobody edits keeps the record entry gave it and is keyed when its cell is next left. A bare `B-roll:` line gets no record, and a seeded `audioRef` is plain text (the #325 "H" findings). A created shot no longer draws a discarded id.
- **Tests.** `src/test/repair-on-read-app.test.mjs` (booted app): render, the badges, both panels, the b-roll refresh, the entry reads and the export kit leave a document whose records lag its script, its generation, edit sequence and dirty state unchanged and publish nothing; a save publishes nothing and changes nothing in the held document; a typed tag and a renamed keyed line carry their records in one transaction, undone together; leaving a cell keys a line in one command with its record; a document the current code wrote enters again with no change. `tests/application/session-records.test.ts`: the hook's fields are declared, a throw in its plan, its apply or the anchor reconcile after it rolls everything back before commit, replay and amend skip it, `noteEdit` runs it and reports a throw. The entry case in `tests/core/migrate.test.ts`. Explained corrections: `broll-id-sequence.test.mjs` and `production-shims.test.mjs` pin entry records, the records plan and read-only reads in place of the read that repaired; `entry-fuzz-app.test.mjs` drops its #325 allowance and flips its three "today" cases; the production goldens drive `derivedRecordsPlan`; `merge-text-unicode-and-atoms.json` now holds the record entry gives its keyed line.

### 6.9 Startup extraction (first PR)

- **Scope.** 6.9's own ledger rows move out of `21-events.js` ahead of the 6.8 feature moves, which they do not depend on. Deleting `21-events.js` waits for 6.8.1–6.8.6 and is the second PR.
- **Owners.** `beginStartup` in `src/bootstrap/startup.ts` orders the startup and owns its lifetime. It calls three owners, each with typed ports: `takeDesktopDraft` (`startup/desktop-draft.ts`), `openInitialDocument` (`startup/initial-document.ts`) and the page-lifetime registrations in `startup/page-lifetime.ts`. `whenDocumentReady` (`startup/dom-ready.ts`) dispatches the startup on DOM ready. The review click gate and its allowlist are `createReviewClickGate` and `REVIEW_SAFE_ACTIONS` in `src/features/review/click-gate.ts`. The first thing `onRootClick` does is call the gate, so it stays in force at the routing boundary while the B rows move. Three legacy owners gain small functions: `loadInitialDocument` in `02b-doc-id.js` (the state reset, then the load), `desktopDraftTaker` in `20g-drafts.js`, and `toastLastEditor`, which moves to `03-sync.js` beside `reportMerge`. `init()` keeps only the port references, and the second PR moves them out with the rest of the file.
- **Ledger rows taken (22).** S01 and B01 go to the click gate. S09, S10 and S25 go to `takeDesktopDraft`: when a draft is pending, it is taken on the next microtask with a failure becoming `null`, otherwise the server is detected at once; after the initial document, the draft is armed, then the server detected, then the draft applied. S14–S24 go to `openInitialDocument`: state reset and load, stash adoption with the cloned new base and reporting off, opening and first draw only when the live document changed, identity chip, autosave menu, hidden-shot menu and save status in that order, the recovery report or the editor toast, last-project resume only in the browser with no report and a never-written document, identity sync, then the identity channel (browser) or the desktop notification (desktop), then history migration. S32 is `startDesktop`, called at `finish()` after those three. S33 and the `DOMContentLoaded` row go to `whenDocumentReady`.
- **Order kept.** The startup begins where the draft acquisition was, opens the document where `S.state = null` was, and finishes where the window registrations were. The listeners registered between those points keep their places until their 6.8 steps take them.
- **Disposal.** `beginStartup` refuses to begin again until the previous startup is disposed, and each phase runs once. `dispose()` removes the three window listeners.
