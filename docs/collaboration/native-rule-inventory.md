This is a rule inventory, not approval to replace the adapter. It covers text, marks, structure, records, undo and recovery on fetched `origin/main` at `39cecc099faf3dfd9c0fcbb33f7b60db3d98f938`. It excludes transport, invitations, persistence, images, file saving, presentation controls and release gates. A refusal that limits editing is included; focus, toast wording and panel layout are not. B-roll text and sound cues are included where they affect text or records.

## Reading the inventory

Each row is one independently reviewable outcome; closely related clauses of a numbered rule are separated where their costs differ. Repeated statements share a row. Later corrections replace earlier answers. A repository-owner comment labelled “assumed for the product owner” remains **assumed**. “Owner” requires a recorded product decision: the Stage 0 decided list, PRs [1383](https://github.com/RetroGorilla/splitpage/pull/1383), [1384](https://github.com/RetroGorilla/splitpage/pull/1384), [1405](https://github.com/RetroGorilla/splitpage/pull/1405), or the explicit decisions in issue comments 61–63. Comments 2, 3 and 5 explicitly decide mark representation; only their stated visible requirements are inventoried, not their chosen representation as a requirement in itself.

**A** means public shared types and `Y.UndoManager` supply the outcome without application history. Ordinary document content, formatting attributes and fields are not “extra” data. **B** means validation, command construction, projection or local undo metadata is needed, with no additional shared history per edit. **C** means the outcome requires retained content or application history beyond ordinary current values; the cost column names it. **D** means the corresponding native operation has a different visible result; its cost column states that result. D is used for direct native counterexamples even when extra history could implement the requested alternative. These are exclusive labels, not additive prices.

“Nothing extra stored” does not mean no native tombstones, formatting items or undo retention. A flag can implement hiding, but a flag alone cannot answer who observed which characters, cancel a matching operation not yet delivered, or distinguish an unseen contribution from the original creation. C does not establish that the current encoding or permanent retention is necessary.

**Checked** refers only to the named two-replica test in [native-rules.test.mjs](../../prototypes/collab/test/native-rules/native-rules.test.mjs), run with Node 24.22.1 and Yjs 13.6.33. **Read** means source reasoning, not execution of the product rule. No application, browser, timing or full regression suite was run for this research. Test names in the evidence column delimit exactly what was checked.

Source shorthand: [Plan](../COLLABORATION-PLAN.md), [S0](stage-0/9-stage-1-experiments.md), [S1](stage-1/1a-decision.md), [Undo](stage-2-shared-undo.md), [Recovery](stage-2-recovery-model.md), [Text](stage-2-text-executor.md), [Structure](stage-2-structure-executor.md), [Joins](stage-2-join-executor.md), [Records](stage-2-record-handlers.md), [Fields](stage-2-record-executor.md), [Merged](stage-2-merged-projection.md), [Groups](stage-2-projection-structure.md), [Buffer](stage-2-buffer.md), [Writers](stage-2-commit-time-writers.md). A source label followed by a heading names that heading in the linked file. `#1414 cN` is the one-based chronological comment number in the fetched issue, not the numbered product rule. The rule-number citations below link directly to the comment, so later additions do not invalidate them.

Native reasoning uses the pinned package's public [Y.Text API](https://github.com/yjs/yjs/blob/v13.6.33/src/types/YText.js), [Y.Map API](https://github.com/yjs/yjs/blob/v13.6.33/src/types/YMap.js), [relative-position API](https://github.com/yjs/yjs/blob/v13.6.33/src/utils/RelativePosition.js), [UndoManager](https://github.com/yjs/yjs/blob/v13.6.33/src/utils/UndoManager.js), and [transaction API](https://github.com/yjs/yjs/blob/v13.6.33/src/utils/Transaction.js). Reading implementation source for analysis is not using private APIs in the scripts. B/C classifications describe required application work, not a proved replacement design.

## Measured costs and scope of the evidence

The [growth/admission record §Results](stage-2-growth-admission.md#results) reports 324,052–325,052 bytes added by 1,000 individual typed characters and 7,894,446–9,131,462 edited-state bytes in its three fixtures. Its correction workload includes deletion undo and redo; it is not a plain type/delete rate. It explicitly says local-input p50, p95 and typing long tasks are **not measured**. Remote admission timings cannot be relabelled as keystroke latency. No supplied external-review timing is treated here as a new measured result.

The [options record §Where one character's bytes go](stage-2-growth-admission-options.md#where-one-characters-bytes-go) partitions its first typed character into **10 native text bytes and 310 birth-record bytes**, total **320**; the anchors account for **145**. Its type/delete probe leaves **328** bytes; one typing undo/redo adds **362**. Bold on two characters adds **278**, its undo another **331**. Its split adds **477**, undo another **416**. These are exact fixture deltas, not unavoidable costs of the rules or independent costs that can be added across rows.

The [same record §Storage alternatives and lifetime](stage-2-growth-admission-options.md#storage-alternatives-and-lifetime) measures **1,017** bytes for 1,000 native characters versus **324,052** for its real individual-key stream, **226,570** with binary anchors, and **155,570** with tuple records. After editing, a new local key costs **5,223–5,224** bytes because it names 100 observed marks; native record references alone lower that probe to **1,833–1,834**. The first probe's rate is therefore not a lifetime bound. Record runs of 10 or 100 characters measured **33,008** or **4,198** bytes per 1,000 characters, trading publication granularity. None of those experiments proves that standard formatting preserves M01–M04 or U02–U04.

Issue #1414 c3 additionally quotes a scratch comparison of about **255 kB** for 1,000 mark commands versus **178 kB** with attributes and about **10 ms** to read 1,000 records without caching. That is quoted scratch evidence, not a measurement made for this inventory and not a Stage 2 production latency figure. The permanent birth/mark design serves several rules together; the sources do not allocate its bytes independently among formatting, undo and recovery. The smaller retained flag demonstrated in R-beat proves availability, not provenance or generation semantics.
