## Mechanisms added

- Both candidates: retained join cancellation ids (`retiredJoins`). Undo retires every matching join known at that point and consumes exactly that command; Bob's later undo cannot reach Alice's bold. Cost: one durable flag per retired join, plus reader filtering and a semantic undo handler. These fixtures cover concurrent identical joins and a remote glue rewrite, not a later intentional rejoin.
- Both candidates: top-item-only native undo. Earlier items are temporarily removed from the public `undoStack` array and restored after `undo()`. Cost: one undo wrapper; the public field has undocumented use (Y7).
- Both candidates: the removal command retains captured observed identities to attribute edits arriving at a removed beat. Components retain their origin and gallery folding is projection-only. Cost: retained observed ranges and a read-time identity membership pass. Split-descendant classification is not established by these fixtures.
- Both candidates: structural selection capture retains endpoint identities, selected identities and the original endpoint affinity. Undo follows restored identities locally, then shares freshly anchored endpoints without following undone deletions. Cost: per-command endpoint/range data and restore work.
